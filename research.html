Research & Projects

Exploring the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and Agriculture. My work focuses on creating sustainable, technology-driven solutions for food systems, autonomous farming rovers, and environmental management.

Current Research Projects

TanPredikt: Time-Series AI Yield Forecasting

Principal Investigator 2022 - 2023

Engineered time-series Machine Learning models (LSTM, Random Forest, XGBoost) coupled with multi-spectral Sentinel-2 and MODIS satellite data to predict rain-fed maize yield across Tanzania prior to harvest, enabling proactive food security planning.

Funding: AI4D Research Grant / IDRC ($12,500)

Related Frontiers in AI Publication (2025)

NAISS-GAI: Automatic Irrigation System

Principal Investigator 2022 - 2024

Designed a Nationwide Automatic Irrigation Scheduling System using Geospatial AI (GeoAI), combining IoT soil moisture telemetry nodes, local weather APIs, and satellite evapotranspiration data to optimize water delivery across Tanzanian irrigation schemes.

Funding: SUARIS Grant (TZS 30M)

Related Publication (2025)

YEESI Lab Ecosystem

PI & Founder 2021 - 2023

Co-founded the Youth Empowerment through Establishment of Social Innovation (YEESI) Lab with University of Georgia partners. Establishes a problem-centered innovation ecosystem training young engineers in computer vision, robotics, and machine learning through hackathons.

Funding: USDA / NIFA / USAID ($157,000)

YEESI Research Website Publication (2024)

EPA Lab Physical AI & Robotics

Lab Founder & Lead 2021 - Present

Founded EPALab (Electronics & Precision Agriculture Lab) at SUA. Leads research in autonomous spraying rovers, drone multi-spectral remote sensing, *Akilimatik* LLM AI Farmers Advisor, *Mwagilia* mobile irrigation app, and IoT telemetry servers.

Funding: TWAS-BMBF Germany (€62,500)

Outsmarting Outbreaks AI Challenge

Principal Investigator 2024 - 2026

Developing predictive machine learning models leveraging Amazon Sustainability Data Initiative (ASDI) satellite environmental features to predict vector-borne infectious disease outbreaks ahead of time, empowering rapid public health intervention.

Funding: Zindi / IRCAI / ASDI ($25,000)

Climate-Sensitive Waterborne Diseases Dataset

co-Principal Investigator 2022 - 2024

Built high-resolution open-access datasets linking climate variables, water quality sensor metrics, and epidemiological data across Tanzanian regions for benchmarking machine learning disease forecasting models.

Funding: Rockefeller Foundation / IDRC / Lacuna Fund ($79,247)

TESTER: Low-Cost AI Soil Device

co-Principal Investigator 2024 - 2026

Developing portable, low-cost spectroscopic hardware embedded with Machine Learning calibration algorithms to deliver instant in-field soil fertility (NPK, pH) diagnostics to smallholder farmers.

Funding: SUARIS Grant (TZS 30M)

Autonomous Cotton Harvesting Robot
